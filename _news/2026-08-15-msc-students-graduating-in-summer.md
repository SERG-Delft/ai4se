Two AI4SE master’s students defended their theses in summer and are now officially alumni. Their projects feed directly into ongoing research streams and several will grow into journal papers in the coming months.

- **Andrei Dragoi** – [*Are AI Agents for Code Robust to Metamorphic Transformations?*](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:5e45141c-95ce-4445-930d-491aaefed5e4) (Track 1).
- **Alexandru Ojica** – [*Mitigating API Hallucinations in Evolving Software Ecosystems with Controlled LoRA Fine-Tuning*](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:f1b6f849-f684-4599-8200-ad542520cad4) (Track 2).

Thank you for your hard work and for broadening AI4SE’s impact — stay in touch, and good luck on your next adventures!
